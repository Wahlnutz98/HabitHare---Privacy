Privacy Policy

HabitHare · Last updated: September 17, 2026

In short: HabitHare does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how HabitHare ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use our iOS application. We built HabitHare to work entirely on your device, without sending your data anywhere.

Information We Collect

HabitHare does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers. We do not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud backends. Any data you enter into the app (such as habits, progress, or settings) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Third-Party Services

HabitHare does not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data, including analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. If this ever changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect it before the change takes effect.

Data Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, we have nothing to share, sell, or disclose to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

HabitHare does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect data from any user.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data lives only on your device. Deleting the app removes all associated data. We have no servers or databases containing your information, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features that involve data collection. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
