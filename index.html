Privacy Policy

HabitHare · Last updated: October 14, 2026

HabitHare stores your habits on your iPhone without sending them to a developer-operated server. We do not run analytics or ads. Device backups can include limited app progress, and you can choose to share a full export. Apple processes purchases and any Health data sharing you permit.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how HabitHare ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use our iOS application. HabitHare tracks habits locally. It has no developer-operated account service or remote habit database. Apple services, your device backups, and exports you choose to share are described below.

Information Stored on Your Device

The HabitHare app does not send us personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers, and we do not use a cloud database for your habit records. What you enter is saved locally and is not transmitted to us by the app. This includes:

  • Your habits, daily check-ins, goals, streaks, custom habits, and challenges
  • Your settings, reminder times, theme, and hare outfit
  • Your dandelion balance, streak freezes, unlocked items, and a record of App Store purchases (used to track purchases and prevent duplicate delivery)

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and part of HabitHare Pro. If you connect it, iOS asks your permission for each kind of data, and HabitHare can only use what you allow:

  • Read: sleep, exercise minutes, workouts, water, and mindful minutes, to fill in today’s check-ins.
  • Write: water and mindful minutes you explicitly log or edit in HabitHare. Imported readings alone do not create Health entries. If a Health update cannot be saved immediately, HabitHare may retry it later.

HabitHare uses Health data locally to display your progress. We do not receive or sell it or use it for advertising or marketing. HabitHare excludes its detailed health records from automatic device backups. Generic activity dates and rewards can be backed up, as explained below. Apple separately controls Health storage and syncing according to your Apple settings. You can change or remove HabitHare’s access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → HabitHare, and turn individual habits off in HabitHare’s Settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Dandelion packs, the Golden Hare, and HabitHare Pro are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your name, payment card, or Apple Account details. The App Store gives the app a signed record of each purchase so it can unlock what you bought, and that record is checked and kept on your device. Subscriptions are managed, cancelled, and refunded through your Apple Account. Apple’s handling of this information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Reminders and Widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; there is no notification server. Home Screen widgets show a summary of your progress, shared with them through storage on your device only.

Third-Party Services

HabitHare does not include any third-party software development kits (SDKs), including analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. It uses only Apple’s own frameworks, such as HealthKit and StoreKit. If this ever changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect it before the change takes effect.

Support and This Website

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so we can respond to your request. HabitHare does not automatically attach your habit or health history to support messages.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits under its Privacy Statement. Visiting this page does not upload your HabitHare records.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data or send habit records to advertisers or data brokers. Information can leave the app through Apple Health when you grant write permission, through your device backups, or through an export you choose to save or share. A full export includes detailed health check-ins. You decide where it goes; the selected destination has its own privacy practices.

Device Backups and Moving to a New iPhone

Eligible iCloud or computer device backups can include general settings, hare appearance, dandelion balances, purchase and refund receipts, overall activity dates used for streaks, generic reward counts, and custom habits you explicitly mark as not containing health information. This progress can reflect app activity without naming a health habit or preserving its measured values.

HabitHare excludes built-in health check-ins, Health connection settings and pending writes, health goals and profile options, detailed challenge identities and history, and health-related custom habits and snapshots from these backups. The local widget cache is also excluded. Custom habits default to containing health information until you change that setting.

Restoring an eligible device backup can recover your general streak and rewards. Health-specific challenge badges and history do not transfer, and some previously rewarded goals or challenge counts cannot earn another bonus. A fresh install without a backup does not recover your history or dandelion balance. Reconnect Apple Health to resume importing today's readings.

Earlier backups created by older versions are not retroactively removed. You control your backups through Apple or your computer. Apple’s Health syncing is separate from HabitHare’s app backup.

Children's Privacy

The HabitHare app does not transmit personal information from users, including children, to us. Please do not send children’s personal information in support messages. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through support.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Delete all my data in Settings erases your profile, habits, check-ins, challenges, and reminders. Your dandelions, streak freezes, unlocked items, and purchase records are kept, so nothing you paid for is lost.
  • Deleting the app removes everything HabitHare stored on your device.
  • Deleting all HabitHare data does not delete Apple Health records. Removing an individual linked check-in can remove its corresponding HabitHare-managed Health entry; legacy and other-source entries remain in Health unless you delete them there.
  • Only the backup-eligible progress described above is included in new HabitHare device backups. Previously created backups and exports may retain information after you delete it from the app.

We do not have a server copy of your habit records to retrieve or delete. If you have sent information to support, contact us about that correspondence separately.
