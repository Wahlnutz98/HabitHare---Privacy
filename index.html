Privacy Policy

HabitHare · Last updated: September 23, 2026

In short: HabitHare doesn’t collect your data. Everything you enter, and anything the app reads from Apple Health, stays on your iPhone. We don’t run servers, analytics, or ads, and we never see your payment details.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how HabitHare ("the app," "we," "us") handles information when you use our iOS application. HabitHare works entirely on your device. Nothing you enter or connect is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information Stored on Your Device

HabitHare does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers, and we do not use cloud backends. What you enter is saved in the app on your iPhone and never transmitted to us or to any third party. This includes:

  • Your habits, daily check-ins, goals, streaks, custom habits, and challenges
  • Your settings, reminder times, theme, and hare outfit
  • Your dandelion balance, streak freezes, unlocked items, and a record of App Store purchases (used so a purchase is never lost or counted twice)

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and part of HabitHare Pro. If you connect it, iOS asks your permission for each kind of data, and HabitHare can only use what you allow:

  • Read: sleep, exercise minutes, workouts, water, and mindful minutes, to fill in today’s check-ins.
  • Write: water and mindful minutes you log in HabitHare, so the Health app stays up to date.

Health data is used only on your device, to show your own progress. It is never sent to us or to any third party, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never stored in iCloud by HabitHare. You can change or remove HabitHare’s access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → HabitHare, and turn individual habits off in HabitHare’s Settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Dandelion packs, the Golden Hare, and HabitHare Pro are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your name, payment card, or Apple Account details. The App Store gives the app a signed record of each purchase so it can unlock what you bought, and that record is checked and kept on your device. Subscriptions are managed, cancelled, and refunded through your Apple Account. Apple’s handling of this information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Reminders and Widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone; there is no notification server. Home Screen widgets show a summary of your progress, shared with them through storage on your device only.

Third-Party Services

HabitHare does not include any third-party software development kits (SDKs), including analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. It uses only Apple’s own frameworks, such as HealthKit and StoreKit. If this ever changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect it before the change takes effect.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers, because it never leaves your device. The only time your data leaves the app is when you choose to export it from Settings and share the file yourself.

Children's Privacy

HabitHare does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, because the app does not collect data from any user.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Delete all my data in Settings erases your profile, habits, check-ins, challenges, and reminders. Your dandelions, streak freezes, unlocked items, and purchase records are kept, so nothing you paid for is lost.
  • Deleting the app removes everything HabitHare stored on your device.
  • Water and mindful minutes HabitHare saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.
  • Like other app data, HabitHare’s data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone.

We have no servers or databases containing your information, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
